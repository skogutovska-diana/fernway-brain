# processes/

How work flows through Fernway, end to end: who does what, in which order, and who it is handed to.

## What belongs here

One process per file, owned by the process owner (proposed):

| Process | Owner |
|---|---|
| New customer onboarding | Lukas Weber |
| Inbound leads | Priya Raman |
| Support escalation | Jonas Berg |
| Billing | Hana Kim |
| Releases | Ivan Petrenko |

Each process doc describes the flow, the hand-offs and known problems. It links to `runbooks/` for detailed steps.

## What does not belong here

- Step-by-step procedures. Those go in `runbooks/`.
- Rules the team disagrees on. Record both versions in `open-questions.md` and link to them.
- Customer names, contacts or data.
