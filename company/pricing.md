# Pricing

## The rules

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| List price | **€39 per venue per month** |
| Annual prepay | **15% discount** |
| Anything below that | A CEO decision: a **Mara-check** |

## Per venue, not per account

- **Billing is per venue.** An account with five venues pays for five venues.
- **Contracts are per account.**
- See *account vs venue* in the [glossary](../glossary.md).

## Pricing exceptions (Mara-check)

- Every pricing exception goes through Mara Voss (CEO).
- This is a known bottleneck.
- How to ask for a Mara-check (channel, what to include, expected turnaround): UNKNOWN — owner: Mara Voss.
