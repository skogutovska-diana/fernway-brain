# Fernway overview

## The company

- Remote-first B2B SaaS, founded 2024.
- Registered in Berlin. The team is spread across five countries.
- **Stage:** seed, raised early 2025.
- **Size:** 15 people plus contractors. See [team-and-owners.md](team-and-owners.md).

## The product

**Fernway Shifts** does shift planning and labour-cost forecasting.

- Managers build schedules.
- The tool checks schedules against local break and overtime rules (see *pay rules* in the [glossary](../glossary.md)).
- It projects the wage bill before the week is published.

## Customer segment

- Independent café and restaurant groups with **3–20 locations**.
- **Markets:** Germany, the Netherlands, Ireland.
- **Size of the base:** about 40 accounts and about 140 paying venues. One account can have many venues; see *account vs venue* in the [glossary](../glossary.md).

## Why customers pick Fernway

- Competitors are built for enterprise chains and take weeks to configure.
- Fernway's pitch: **a venue is live in under a day.**
- Onboarding speed is the whole positioning. That is why **T2L** (time to live) is the number the team watches.
- How onboarding works today: [processes/customer-onboarding.md](../processes/customer-onboarding.md).
