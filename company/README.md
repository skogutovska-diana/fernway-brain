# company/

Facts about Fernway as a company.

## What belongs here

- What Fernway is: product, customers, stage, positioning.
- Pricing rules: list price, discounts, who approves exceptions. Owner: Mara Voss (proposed).
- Team and ownership: who does what, and who owns which process and doc.

One topic per file, e.g. `pricing.md`, `team.md`.

## What does not belong here

- Compensation, salary bands or other people data.
- Undecided commercial matters, such as accounts whose pricing is still being decided.
- Customer names linked to personal contacts.
